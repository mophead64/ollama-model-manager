What quantisation names mean

Quantising stores a model's weights with fewer bits, so it takes less memory and disk and usually runs faster, at some cost in accuracy. The name says how it was done. Take Q4_K_M:

  • Q4: about 4 bits per weight. The fewer the bits, the smaller the model and the more it loses.
  • K: the method. See below.
  • M: the size variant: how many of its layers are kept at higher precision.

Bits

F16 BF16 F32Unquantised: the original quality, at 4× (F32: 8×) the size of 4-bit. Rarely worth it to run.
Q8Practically indistinguishable from the original, at about twice the size of 4-bit.
Q6 Q5Very close to the original. Worth it if they fit.
Q4The usual choice: a good balance of size and quality. Q4_K_M is what Ollama's own tags usually are.
Q3Noticeably less accurate. For fitting a bigger model than would otherwise fit.
Q2 Q1A big loss in quality: a last resort. A smaller model at Q4 is often better than a bigger one at Q2.

Methods

_K (k-quants)The usual kind: quantised in blocks, with the parts that matter most kept more precise. Q4_K_M, Q5_K_S, Q6_K.
_0 _1 (legacy)The older, simpler method. A k-quant of the same size is usually better, except Q8_0, the usual 8-bit format.
IQ (i-quants)Use an “importance matrix” to squeeze more quality into fewer bits, so they're best at 3 bits and below (IQ3_XXS, IQ2_M). They can be slower on a CPU.
MXFP44-bit floating point: the format some models, such as gpt-oss, are released in.
TQ1_0 TQ2_0Ternary: only for models trained to run that way.

Size variants

XXS, XS, S, M, L and XL go from smallest to largest. For the same bits, a larger variant keeps more layers precise: a little bigger, a little better. M is the usual balance. Uploaders sometimes add letters of their own (such as _P); those are their variants, so see the repo's description.

Prefixes and tags

UD-Unsloth Dynamic: picks the precision layer by layer, so it's better than a plain quant of the same size.
qatQuantisation-aware trained (Gemma's -it-qat tags): trained to be run at 4 bits, so it loses less than an ordinary Q4.
it instructNot a quantisation: the instruction-tuned (chat) version, as opposed to the base model.

Rule of thumb: pick the largest quant that fits on your GPU, Q4_K_M or better. Discover's ✓ says which do.
